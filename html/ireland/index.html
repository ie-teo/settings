Dublin

The capital of Ireland

Sight

Dublin is the capital of Ireland and a beautiful city located on the east coast of the country, beside the River Liffey. It is one of the most famous cities in Ireland and combines a rich history, old architecture, and modern city life.


cIn Dublin, you can see historic buildings, old streets, beautiful parks, museums, libraries, and famous attractions.


The city is also known for its friendly neighborhoods, cafés, restaurants, and traditional Irish pubs. Dublin attracts tourists from all over the world and is an important cultural, educational, and economic center of Ireland.
